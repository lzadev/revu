# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in revu, whether as a user, a reporter or a contributor, to be a welcoming and harassment-free
experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new to the project or to the topic.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences, and giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and the community.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other place where someone
represents the project) and applies to everyone who takes part.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments, commits,
issues and other contributions that do not follow them, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they
consider inappropriate.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@lzadev](https://github.com/lzadev), through GitHub. For sensitive reports you can
use a [private security advisory](https://github.com/lzadev/revu/security/advisories/new) and mention that it is a conduct
report. All reports are handled confidentially and fairly.
